Microsoft static runtime and Rust dependency notices

p4kx-msi-path.exe is built with Rust's MSVC target and crt-static enabled.
Its actual target, compiler, binary hash, imports and dependency SBOM are recorded
in p4kx-msi-helper.json and p4kx-msi-helper.cdx.json. The helper runs from the MSI
Binary stream and requires no installed P4KX application or app-local CRT DLL.
