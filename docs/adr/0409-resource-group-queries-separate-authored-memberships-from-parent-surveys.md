# Resource group queries separate authored declarations from parent surveys

The selective SDK exposes the unchanged definitions.resourceGroups and
declarations.resourceGroupMembers through with_resource_groups and its decoded-entry/session
forms. These two datasets contain facts from the selected physical owners: direct scalar cells,
public names, protected descriptions and both original membership arrays. Resource bodies are
not required to retain their authored references. A reference's source-resolution status remains
separate from the Catalog's domain-family health policy.

The parent projection has a separate with_resource_group_parents endpoint. A parent can be
outside the requested child-owner selection, and proving no parent requires surveying the
canonical ResourceTypeGroup family. That survey follows actual physical Record order and uses
the same Parents/ParentSource interpreter as Catalog production. GUID/physical selection limits
returned children; it does not filter possible parent owners. This endpoint reads no Resource or
World bodies and no locale payloads, including when invoked through a localized parsed session.

An eligible successful selected owner is required before dependency work. Selected trees and
known failed owner reads are reused. Other admitted group trees are consumed and released during
the survey. Original source slots, physical aliases, cycles and repeated references remain
unchanged. The chosen parent is the first selected group that refers to the child's GUID and
has a different GUID; another physical owner of the same GUID does not become a parent.
Output sorting by textual GUID and physical index happens after selection.

Parent source knowledge retains the actual prefix through the winning slot. An earlier
unreadable possible candidate makes the choice incomplete; a later fault does not become a
counterfactual contributor. No-parent knowledge requires the complete relevant survey. Known
foreign headers are excluded by the existing canonical metadata-failure interpreter. Unreadable
headers/bodies retain their real physical observations and attempted inputs, without inventing
known group identities from requested GUIDs or metadata DTOs.

The canonical definition reader is shared with production, while direct membership preparation
uses the existing source-owned reader on that same once-built owner tree. Their OriginalFlowFields
preparations remain sequential; one body read does not imply one field traversal or constant
memory. The kind-generic owner planner lives under the shared Record query helper and is reused
by mining and group APIs. No duplicate decoder or serialized adapter is introduced.

All existing serialized fields have immutable typed getters. Direct text and SourceInventory
references resolve only inside their result context. Parent results have their own source
inventory and explicit owner/dependency coverage. The direct snapshot does not expose an empty
parent list as a claim that a parent projection ran. Callback success is not complete-source
proof: consumers examine coverage and cells, then publish only after outer success and its
Archive/cancellation checks. Schema v3 fields, field meanings and source axes remain unchanged.
