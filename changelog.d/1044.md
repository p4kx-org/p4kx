### Added

- Basetool catalog: `resourceTypes` exports resource definitions independently of commodities,
  including resources with no commodity. Commodity GUIDs remain the primary identity;
  `subtypeGuid` is a nullable many-to-one resource link. Resource groups preserve descriptions,
  thumbnail paths and all authored membership links. Filtered diagnostic reads cannot change
  the selected groups used by legacy `typeGuid` and `parentGuid`.
