### Added

- The Archive reader retains exact directory DOS date/time words for v1, v2 and ZIP
  Entries, including zero and invalid encodings, with a separate validated calendar
  view that implies no timezone or payload-integrity result.
- Archive media records include the shared physical-owner and raw DOS timestamp
  metadata object, with explicit calendar validity and a precise built-in schema.
- The Archive reader exposes the original directory position, raw compression
  method, ZIP version/flag/disk/attribute words and v2 encryption word. Positions
  survive folder admission; recovery-scanned records keep an unknown position.
