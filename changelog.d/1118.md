### Added

- Archive key: a command that asked for encrypted Entries it could not read, because no key was
  given or the key does not fit, prints one note on stderr that says how many and how to give the
  key. The `source` block of the catalog and of the ship base states `archiveKey` (`given`,
  `missing` or `wrong`) and `encryptedEntriesUnread`, and `p4kx catalog --json` reports both.
  `Archive::key_demand` counts the Entries for library callers.
